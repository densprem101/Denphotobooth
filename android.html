How to install

  1. Download the file (or scan the QR code). If your browser asks, choose Keep or Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file (check your Downloads folder or the browser's download notification).
  3. If Android says "For your security, your phone isn't allowed to install unknown apps from this source", tap Settings and switch on Allow from this source for your browser (Chrome, Samsung Internet, etc.). On some phones this is under Settings → Apps → Special access → Install unknown apps.
  4. Go back and tap Install. If Google Play Protect shows a warning because the app isn't from the Play Store, tap More details then Install anyway.
  5. Open Den Collective and allow the camera when asked, so the photo booth can take your pictures.

Using an iPhone, iPad or computer? APK files only work on Android. Just open denphotobooth.online in your browser, no download needed.

Stay safe: only install the app from this page. After installing, you can switch "install unknown apps" back off.
