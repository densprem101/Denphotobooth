Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 5, 2026

Welcome to Den Collective Photobooth ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website denphotobooth.online.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways, including:

  • Personal Data: When you sign in using Google or other authentication providers, we collect basic profile details such as your name, email address, and profile picture solely for authentication and session management.
  • Photobooth Data: Photos, custom templates, and session configurations you create or interact with while using our platform, which may be synced across your devices.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you access and use the website, including your browser type, device details, and interaction logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

Having accurate information permits us to provide you with a smooth, efficient, and customized experience. Specifically, we use information collected to:

  • Create and manage your user account and authenticate your identity securely.
  • Enable cloud synchronization of your photobooth sessions and preferences across multiple devices.
  • Improve website performance, features, and user experience.
  • Respond to customer service requests and support inquiries.

3. Disclosure of Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties, except to provide our services (such as secure hosting providers and authentication services like Google) or when required by law.

4. Security of Your Information

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures designed to help protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

5. Cookies and Sessions

When you sign in, we use a secure session cookie to keep you signed in on your device. We use it only to recognize your session. Signing in with Google uses Google's own sign-in page, and we never see your Google password.

6. Where Your Photos and Settings Are Stored

Your photos, layouts, templates, scheduled events and welcome screen settings are kept in your own browser on your device. If you sign in, they can also be synced to your account in our cloud storage, so you can open them on another device. You can delete the data stored in your account from inside the app.

7. Your Choices

You can sign out at any time, remove photos and templates you no longer want, and clear this site's data in your browser settings. If you want help with your account or your data, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our website at denphotobooth.online.
